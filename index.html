Questions, answered clearly.

Practical answers about Flowbase—how it works, who it’s for, and what to expect before you start.

Flowbase is a focused project management workspace. Teams use it to organize projects, assign ownership, track deadlines, and keep conversations connected to the work—without juggling multiple tools.

Flowbase is built for product, engineering, operations, and cross-functional teams that need clear ownership and visible priorities. It works well for startups and growing companies that want structure without heavy process.

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start, so your team can explore projects, tasks, and collaboration before choosing a plan.

Flowbase offers Starter, Professional, and Business plans billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing saves 20%. You can compare plan limits and features in the pricing section, then upgrade as your team grows.

Invite teammates to a shared workspace, assign owners to tasks, and keep discussion next to the work itself. Everyone sees the same priorities, progress, and deadlines—so status updates stay lightweight and useful.

Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by role-based permissions, and Business plans include admin controls and SSO. We continuously monitor systems and follow industry security practices.
